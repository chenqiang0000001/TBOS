## EPD：2026年第三季度

公告日期：2026-10-29。

原文：https://ir.enterpriseproducts.com/news-releases/news-release-details/enterprise-reports-third-quarter-2026-earnings

直接核对的原文摘录：

> Operational DCF: $2.3 billion, which provided 1.9x coverage of distributions declared for 3Q 2026, up 27%; retained $1.1 billion of DCF

> Common unit repurchases: $112 million for the quarter and $437 million for the 12 months ended September 30, 2026; 36% cumulative utilization of $5.0 billion buyback program

> For the 12 months ended September 30, 2026, the payout ratio, comprised of distributions to common unitholders and common unit buybacks, was 54% of Adjusted CFFO

> Distributions declared: $0.56 per common unit, or $2.24 per common unit annualized, up 2.8%

附表核对：9个月GAAP经营现金流6,835百万美元；资本开支3,169百万美元；最近12个月Adjusted FCF 5,799百万美元。季度稀释平均单位数2026年2,181百万，2025年2,186百万。Operational DCF包含收回以前期间确认的应收账款100百万美元。非GAAP覆盖率不是扣除所有增长资本开支后的现金结余，不能与其他公司同名指标直接排名。

分派历史：https://ir.enterpriseproducts.com/distribution-drip

该发行人网站提示历史数据由Mergent提供。2023年度分派1.98美元，2024年2.08美元，2025年2.16美元。2026年最新季度0.56美元；2026-11-13预定支付的一笔截至核对日尚未支付。

截至2026-11-05，过去12个月已支付四笔合计：0.545+0.55+0.55+0.56=2.205美元。用页面按除息日列示、包含尚未支付金额的年度合计，不能冒称已收现金。

## MPLX：2026年第三季度

公告日期：2026-11-03。

原文：https://ir.mplx.com/CorporateProfile/press-releases/news-release/2026/MPLX-LP-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results-Announces-Increase-to-Quarterly-Distribution-of-12-5/default.aspx

直接核对的原文摘录：

> Increased quarterly distribution 12.5% for the third consecutive year, to $4.84 per unit annualized

> During the quarter, MPLX generated $1,477 million in net cash provided by operating activities, $1,537 million of distributable cash flow, and adjusted free cash flow of $447 million. MPLX announced a third-quarter 2026 distribution of $1.2111 per common unit, resulting in distribution coverage of 1.3x for the quarter. The leverage ratio was 3.8x at the end of the quarter.

> The partnership repurchased $50 million of common units held by the public in the third quarter of 2026.
