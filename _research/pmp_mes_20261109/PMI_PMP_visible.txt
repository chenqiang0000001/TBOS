Is a PMP right for you?

The PMP certification proves you have the project leadership and expertise in any way of working: predictive, hybrid or agile. Unlike other certifications, PMP demonstrates your ability to lead projects without being tied to any specific methodology, industry or geography.

That’s why employers around the world recognize PMP as the gold standard certification in Project Professional certifications. And, why PMP holders earn more, on average, than non-certified colleagues.

Check your eligibility

Part of the reason PMP is the gold standard is because professionals need to meet strict criteria before they can take the certification exam.

Before you apply, make sure you meet one of the following sets of PMP certification requirements, beginning with the type of education and experience you have, then training.

Not sure you qualify?

Answer a few questions about your education and experience to find out if you’re ready to apply for the PMP. Or find another PMI certification that fits you better.

Education and Experience Requirements

You’ll need ONE of the following:

High School Diploma

(or GED, Secondary/Upper School Certificate)

Plus: 5 years of experience managing projects

Associate’s Degree or Vocational Trade School Diploma

Plus: 4 years of experience managing projects

Bachelor’s Degree or Higher

Plus: 3 years of experience managing projects

PMI GAC Program

Bachelor’s or Post-Graduate Degree from a PMI Global Accreditation Center Program

Plus: 2 years of experience managing projects

What counts as project management experience?

Add up time that you managed projects in a professional setting—taking responsibility for planning, decisions, delivery and outcomes—within the last 10 years. Don’t double-count time for projects that overlapped.

You'll list each project separately on the PMP application.

Need more details? Read our "How to Apply for the PMP" blog post.

Project Professional Training Requirements

You will need 35 hours of Project Professional training via ONE of the following:

Online, on-demand class

Learn on your own schedule with an online, on-demand course.

Project Management Professional (PMP)® Exam Prep Course
Self-paced courses from any organization
Live, instructor-led class

Starting 1 December 2026, live classes (taught by an instructor in-person or online) are eligible only if delivered by a:

PMI Authorized Training Partner (ATP) or
China Registered Education Provider (REP) or
Eligible, accredited higher education program (including PMI GAC programs)
Active Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM)® Certification

An active CAPM certification also fulfills PMP training requirements.

What to Expect on Exam Day

First, congratulations!

You’re one step closer to advancing your career. Once your application is accepted, you’ll pay your exam fee and schedule your exam.

Here’s what to expect on exam day:

Format: 180 questions
Time: You’ll have 4 hours to complete the exam
Associated exam content: People 33%, Process 41%, Business Environment 26%
Breaks: Includes two 10-minute breaks
Delivery Options: You can take your exam in person at a Pearson Vue Testing Center (recommended) or securely online
If testing online, be sure to run a system test, choose a private space and have a valid photo ID ready. Learn how to prepare

Maintaining your PMP certification

Earning your certification is a big step.
Maintaining it shouldn’t be.

Continuing education is core to PMI certification. PMP holders need to earn 60 professional development units (PDUs) in each three-year renewal cycle to maintain certification.

Each PDU represents one hour spent:

Learning
Teaching others
Presenting
Reading
Volunteering
Content creating
