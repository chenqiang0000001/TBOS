Environmental, Social, Governance
“At P&G, we believe business success is inextricably linked to the well-being of the broader community. This is far more profound than just “doing good.” It is an integrated commitment to corporate citizenship that spans every facet of our operations. This creates a virtuous cycle, where a thriving society supports a thriving business. This growth fuels our ability to invest in our talented people, research and development programs, brand-building activities, and operations — enabling us to deliver sustainable, profitable growth that creates value for all our stakeholders: consumers, customers, employees, society and shareowners.”
Shailesh G. Jejurikar Chairman of the Board, President and Chief Executive Officer
