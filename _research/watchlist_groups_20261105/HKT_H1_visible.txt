HKT reports solid interim results for 2026

HKT (SEHK: 6823) – HONG KONG, 29 July 2026 – The directors of HKT Management Limited (in its capacity as the trustee-manager of the HKT Trust) (the “Trustee-Manager”) and HKT Limited (the “Company” or “HKT”) are pleased to announce the unaudited consolidated results of the HKT Trust and of the Company together with the Company’s subsidiaries (collectively the “Group”) for the six months ended 30 June 2026. The key highlights are as follows:

Underpinned by our comprehensive business portfolio, artificial intelligence (AI)-ready infrastructure, disciplined execution and relentless focus on innovation, HKT’s key business segments maintained steady growth in the first half of 2026.

The robust growth in our enterprise business and increased broadband and mobile revenue contributed to a total revenue growth of 8% to HK$18,685 million; total revenue excluding Mobile product sales rose by 3% to HK$16,831 million.

The Group continued to integrate AI across its operations, enhancing efficiency and productivity, resulting in over 3% growth in total EBITDA to HK$6,586 million. Alongside prudent capital management, adjusted funds flow (“AFF”) also climbed by 3% to HK$2,639 million.

Profit attributable to holders of Share Stapled Units increased by 4% to HK$2,153 million. The Board of Directors has recommended an interim distribution of 34.80 HK cents per Share Stapled Unit.

Leveraging the Group’s unparallelled network assets, well-established distribution capabilities, and long-standing, trusted customer relationships, HKT is well positioned to capture growth in the AI era – as an infrastructure provider, AI aggregator, and AI enabler – to drive monetisation.

In terms of network infrastructure, the Group plans to roll out its 3.2Tbps AI Data Centre Inter-connect Superhighway, which will run on the advanced hollow core fiber, to major data centre clusters across Hong Kong over the next 3 years. In the first phase this year, the network will connect the Lok Ma Chau Loop cluster with the Tseung Kwan O cluster. Coverage will be extended to Fo Tan and Kwai Chung next year, and subsequently to the Sandy Ridge cluster once it becomes operational, providing access to computing resources across multiple data centres. The related infrastructure investment will be supported by pre-funded capex from anchor customers, with over HK$100 million already secured and further pipelines under discussion.

The Group continued to assist enterprise customers with their AI transformation through the implementation of our AI-powered solutions across multiple industries. As a result, our enterprise business maintained robust growth in the first half of 2026, with revenue increasing by 8% year-on-year, while also securing new project wins with the total contract value exceeding HK$2.2 billion. The Group also continued to capture the growth opportunities in the Chinese Mainland and the Greater Bay Area, driven by sustained growth in our business from Chinese Mainland enterprise customers, with revenue expanding by 6% year-on-year.

Demand for International Telecommunications Services remained strong, driving a 2% increase in revenue. Demand from Chinese Mainland OTT customers recorded the most notable growth, with related revenue rising by over 50%.

In our consumer business, broadband revenue grew by 3% year-on-year, as demand for our high-speed, ultra-low-latency fibre services remained strong. Customers continued to upgrade to 2500M broadband service, reflecting growing demand to support entertainment, hybrid work, smart home applications and AI-powered services. The 2500M customer segment grew by 68% to 129,000 subscribers, generating an ARPU uplift of around HK$70. Penetration of the 2500M customer segment has now reached almost 12% of our fibre-to-the-home (“FTTH”) base.

Mobile services revenue also continued to deliver growth, increasing by 5% to HK$4,394 million. 5G post-paid customer base grew by 16% year-on-year, reaching 2.2 million at the end of June 2026, representing 62% of our total post-paid base, driven by our upgraded network and AI-driven data usage. Customer retention remained strong during the period, with the post-paid churn rate of our core 1O1O and csl customer base further improving to 0.6%. Benefitted from strong outbound travel and increasing visitor arrivals to Hong Kong, consumer outbound roaming and inbound roaming revenue increased by 11% and 28% respectively in the first half of the year, resulting in total roaming revenue growth of 8% year-on-year.

Now TV’s highlight for the first half was the exclusive broadcast of the FIFA World Cup 2026™. Despite the challenging match times, consumer sales of event passes exceeded the previous edition by more than 18%, with nearly 20% purchased by non-HKT customers. The total installed base of Now TV expanded by 3%, while the OTT customer base increased by 18%.

The Group showcased the strength of our quad-play capabilities through tailored content and service offerings, with conversion rates for upselling and cross-selling improving significantly during the period. The number of customers using multiple HKT services with subscriptions to three or more products recorded a year-on-year increase of 20%.

The newly launched HKT.AI, a one-stop, unified AI platform that enables individuals and SME customers to access multiple leading third-party AI models through a single interface. The service can be bundled will our core plans to strengthen customer relationships, drive ARPU uplift and enhance customer stickiness, ultimately increasing customer lifetime value while powering our next growth lever.

Susanna Hui, HKT Group Managing Director, said, “As we usher in the AI era, over the past six months, HKT accelerated the adoption of AI across its network and operations and integrated it into services for both consumer and enterprise customers to boost productivity and service quality. We will continue to leverage our existing assets and capabilities to reinforce our position at the core of the AI ecosystem and unlock new growth opportunities. We will serve as both a trusted aggregator of AI services and an enabler of AI-powered solutions, drawing on the expertise gained from our internal AI deployments. The Group has delivered a strong start to the year. Barring any unforeseen circumstances, I am confident that the momentum seen in our business will drive strong results for the full year.”

For details of the 2026 interim results, please refer to the announcement that has been filed with The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited.
